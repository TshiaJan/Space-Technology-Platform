## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **AI Space Technology Platform** ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to providing comprehensive space mission, orbital mechanics, and satellite telemetry simulation while maintaining strict user privacy and transparency.

By accessing or using the AI Space Technology Platform, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

AI Space Technology Platform is built as a client-side, offline-capable aerospace simulation interface. We follow a strict data-minimization approach:

- **No Personal Identifiable Information (PII):** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
- **No Mandatory User Accounts:** You can explore satellite constellations, calculate orbital trajectories, and monitor missions without signing up or creating an account.
- **No Precise Location Tracking:** We do not track, log, or store your GPS or geographical location.
- **No Biometric Data:** We do not collect facial data, fingerprints, or biological measurements.
- **No Camera or Microphone Access:** The Application does not record audio, capture images, or access your device cameras.
- **No Contact Lists or Private Files:** We do not inspect your device contacts, photo library, or personal files.

---

## 3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All telemetry filters, bookmarked satellites, configured mission parameters, and application preferences are saved locally on your device via standard client-side storage (`localStorage`):

- **Selected Satellites & Fleet Watchlists**
- **Orbital Calculation Parameters & Custom Trajectories**
- **Mission Progress & Simulation Bookmarks**
- **User Interface Preferences (Theme, Navigation state, Tab preferences)**

All data remains entirely on your device and is never transmitted to external servers. You can clear this data at any time through your device or browser settings.

---

## 4. Simulated Aerospace Operations

The satellite tracking, trajectory planning, orbital mechanics, and mission telemetry within the Application are simulated for educational, operational modeling, and entertainment purposes. No classified orbital data or restricted aerospace systems are accessed.

## 5. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The core application functions offline without transmitting telemetry data. If distributed builds include standard developer frameworks or advertising platforms (such as Google Play Services or AdMob), those services may automatically collect standard diagnostic details:

- Device model, operating system version, and general system performance data.
- Anonymous Advertising Identifier (e.g., Google Advertising ID), subject to your device privacy controls.
- Crash logs and diagnostic data to improve app stability.

These third parties process information in accordance with their own independent privacy policies.
